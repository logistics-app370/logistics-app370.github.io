Open Logistics

Tap Open Logistics to continue in Safari. That keeps Google sign-in working.

Add to Home Screen

  1. Tap the Share button (the square with the arrow).
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. If you see Open as Web App, turn it off so the icon opens in Safari and sign-in works.
  4. Name it Logistics and tap Add.
